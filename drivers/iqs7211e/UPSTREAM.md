# IQS7211E driver

This directory vendors `sekigon-gonnoc/zmk-driver-iqs7211e` at commit
`436d3c42172abf812ec104521f29384fc02fc50e`.

Local changes replace the one-finger click and double-tap-drag behavior with
single- and double-tap navigation marker events. The central half maps those
markers to `Alt+Left` and `Alt+Right`.
